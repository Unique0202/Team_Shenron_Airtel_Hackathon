## (b) Where we win

- **One advisor, ten languages, one bundle.** No telco lets a kirana owner or clinic describe their business in Hindi or Tamil and get a priced, mixed bundle (BB/5G FWA + SIMs + WhatsApp + email + security + CCTV + POS). Rivals offer assessments (Vi), a mascot (TTBS) or a callback form (Jio, Airtel, TTBS).
- **Self-checkout where telcos stop at "Request a callback".** Every telco journey above ends in a sales callback (Airtel: within 1 business day; Vi: within 48 h). We close the simple cases online and pass only complex leads to field sales or partners, with full context.
- **Registry-backed KYC.** GSTIN or Udyam lookup, document reading and e-sign replace "keep originals ready at install" (Jio) and "assisted doorstep KYC" (Vi). It also builds DoT business-connection compliance into the flow [37].
- **One transparent bill with add-ons you can toggle.** Today an MSME pays 4–6 vendors: ISP, telco, WhatsApp provider, Workspace, POS, CCTV. Only Airtel owns enough of that stack (network, SIMs, WhatsApp provider status, Workspace and security partners) to put it on one bill.
- **Order tracker covering connectivity and SaaS.** No competitor shows one live status across fibre install, SIM KYC and app activation.

## (c) Threats: what competitors do better

- **Jio wins on price.** ₹801 for 100 Mbps with cloud storage and a landline, and installation waived on quarterly plans, sets the price expectation. Our bundle must show total value against 4–6 separate bills, not compete on headline broadband price.
- **TTBS is moving into "advisor + AI" first.** It has the Mira advisor (2025), voice-AI agents with a business number (Jul 2026), a cloud marketplace and an SMB channel network. Single-product SaaS (Wati, Interakt, MyOperator, Workspace) already gives truly instant sign-up with free trials and deeper features than a telco bundle.
- **Regulation limits "instant" for SIMs.** DoT rules (since Oct 2023) keep business SIMs inactive until each end user completes KYC that matches the business's records [37]. Vi was flagged for lapses [9]. Our "near-instant" claim must mean instant entity KYC plus guided end-user e-KYC, not skipping it.
